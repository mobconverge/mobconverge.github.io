We have worked with virtual, as well as digitally integrated physical products that have launched in Fortune-500 companies and startups. This variety of experience can help your idea blossom into a reality.

## From idea to shipped product

The hard part of a new product is rarely the code. It is deciding what to build first, what to leave out, and how to learn whether the thing is working before the runway is gone. Having done this on both sides — inside Fortune-500 organizations and from a standing start at a startup — we bring the pattern recognition that shortens that loop.

## The range of experience

- Virtual products, delivered entirely in software
- Digitally integrated physical products, where hardware and software have to land together
- Launches inside Fortune-500 companies, with the constraints that come with them
- Startups building a first product with everything still to prove

## A partner, not just a developer

Clients bring Mark in when they need more than a developer — they need a partner who can own outcomes end to end. That means being in the conversation about what the product should be, not only how to build it.

Mark has partnered with brands like Emerson and MLS club St. Louis CITY SC, and has led engineering for products used by millions — including apps recognized by the White House as a Champion of Change and a connected-home product ranked highest in customer satisfaction by J.D. Power.

Whether you are a startup building your first product or an established team raising the bar, we work alongside you to deliver software that is built right the first time.
