We use AI tools to move faster without cutting corners — every line is reviewed, tested, and owned by an experienced engineer. You get the speed of AI-assisted development with the judgment and accountability of someone who's shipped production software for two decades.

## Speed without the cleanup bill

AI tooling has made it dramatically cheaper to produce code. It has not made it cheaper to produce code that is correct, maintainable, and safe to change. The gap between those two things is where projects quietly go wrong — a codebase that grew fast, that nobody fully understands, and that resists every change you need to make next.

Our approach is expert-driven and AI-assisted, in that order. Modern AI tooling accelerates the work, but every decision is still made and owned by an experienced engineer.

## What that looks like in practice

- AI used where it is genuinely faster — scaffolding, refactors, test coverage, exploration
- Every line reviewed by an engineer who can explain why it is there
- The same test-driven discipline applied to AI-generated code as to hand-written code
- Architecture and design decisions made by a human who is accountable for them

## Why accountability matters

When something breaks in production at two in the morning, the question is not which tool wrote the code. It is whether anyone understands it. We take that ownership: the design decisions are ours to defend, the tests are ours to have written, and the result is a codebase your team can pick up and carry forward.
